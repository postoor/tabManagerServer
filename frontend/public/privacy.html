Privacy Policy

Tab Manager Chrome Extension  ·  Last updated: March 2026

Overview

Tab Manager is a browser extension that helps you save, organize, and sync your browser tabs and bookmarks. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and your rights.

Data We Collect

Locally (no account required)

  • Tab and bookmark data — collection titles, tab URLs and titles you choose to save. Stored only in your browser via chrome.storage.local.
  • Language preference — your chosen display language. Stored locally.

With a sync account (optional)

  • Email address — used as your account identifier.
  • Password — stored as a bcrypt hash; we never store plaintext passwords.
  • Bookmark and collection data — synced to your self-hosted or chosen server to enable cross-device access.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Browsing history beyond what you explicitly save
  • Keystrokes, form data, or any content from web pages
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or analytics
  • Any data from third parties

How Your Data Is Used

  • To display and organize your saved tabs within the extension
  • To sync your collections across your own devices when you opt in
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party

Sync Server

The sync feature is entirely optional. If you choose to use it, you connect to a server of your choice (including a self-hosted instance). Your data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored on that server only. We have no access to privately hosted servers.

Permissions Used

  • tabs — to read open tab URLs and titles so you can save them to collections.
  • storage — to persist your collections locally in the browser.
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>) — required to fetch favicons and to communicate with the sync server URL you configure.

Data Deletion

You can delete all local data at any time by removing the extension or clearing its storage via chrome://extensions. If you created a sync account, you can delete your account and all associated data by contacting us or through the web interface.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the extension evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the project repository or contact us via the Chrome Web Store listing.
